Answer an AI employee's questions in the task sheet and see everything that needs you on the Tasks page. Each open question has suggested answers, a text box and a "this changes what was agreed" switch; a task's history becomes a conversation (employee updates, questions, answers, people's actions) with command-level detail folded away. A "Needs your attention" list shows open questions and runs that stopped.
